## Recording tips
- Use a phone in a quiet room with soft furnishings (a bedroom works well), 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
- Record the whole script 2–3 times in one take; I'll choose the best one and line it up with the subtitles.
- Send it as WAV or M4A, not a WhatsApp voice note (those are heavily compressed). If WhatsApp is the only option, send it as a "document".
